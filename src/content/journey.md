# Journey

**Last updated:** 11/23/26

Notes on my tennis journey (from what I remember)

## High School

**Date:** Before May 2024

Played 1-2 times during junior year and 2-3 times during junior summer. I was not good, struggled to hit the ball in, and was worried each time I swung my forehand.

## Freshman Year 

**Date:** June 2024 - April 2025

Played 4-5 times maybe. Tried the indoor courts and Fannie Mae Dees courts. From what I remember I felt like my forehands were definitely improving but were super inconsistent. I switched between using the Walmart Racket and T's Wilson. 

## Freshman-Sophomore Summer

**Date:** Summer 2025

This was the first period of time where I played semi-consistently (31 sessions over 14 weeks, or ~2.2 sessions per week). I played a lot with high school friends and primarily used the Wilson US Open.  

I also played a set during the summer and lost badly (6-1) which told me I could not hit the ball consistently.

## Sophomore Year

**Date:** August 2025 - April 2026

During this year I played with much better players, which helped me improve my forehand consistency. I was told my forehand actually exists, which was a good sign. I still didn't trust my backhand at all.

## Sophomore-Junior Summer

**Date:** Summer 2026

I played a lot of tennis this summer - 1-2 times per week. I tried using Grace's racket and felt a noticeable improvement in my shots (so I thought maybe my past rackets were holding me back a little). For a few sessions (but never consistently), I felt my backhand was in a good place. I also restrung the Wilson US Open racket but it made me play worse. The strings definitely felt worse. 

## Junior Year

**Date:** August 2026 - 

I started playing with the Wilson Blade and once again felt a noticeable improvement in my shots, and in particular, felt like I could produce spin on my strokes (primarily the forehand). Over the span of 2-3 months, I felt ever few sessions or so I would feel like I had improved a good amount, and Miles seemed to slightly agree. I tried Miles' Babolat once, felt like I played the best tennis of life on first use, and then regressed the next time I used it and switched back to the Wilson. 

# Rackets

## Wilson Pro Staff 110 Precision XL
A nice red racket. Obtained from an uncle, according to my dad. It felt pretty nice, just heavy. The head size was also pretty big (which I didn't realize at the time). The strings on this racket snapped sometime during the summer before freshman year or during freshman year (I forgot). 

I wrapped my first overgrip on this racket and it was super cool.

## Walmart Racket
Cheap racket probably from Walmart. This was functioning racket we had at home, so it was what I used. 

## Wilson US Open BLX 100

Gifted to me by T. Very light, felt great initially. I was between using this racket vs. the Wilson Pro Staff for a while. The strings got worse, so I replaced them with Solinco Pro Stacked synthetic gut, but those definitely did not feel good and are what's on the racket right now.

## Grace's Babolat
I used this for about a summer, and it was pretty nice. 

## Wilson Blade
Good weight, strings (Hyper-G). So far I don't think I have any complaints.
